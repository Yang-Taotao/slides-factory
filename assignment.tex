\section{Math}
\label{sec:math}
    Write numbered equations with labelling quickly with:
    \begin{lstlisting}[language=tex]
    \[ 
        E = m c^2
        \label{eq:einstein}
    \]
    \end{lstlisting}
    This becomes:
    \[ 
        E = m c^2
        \label{eq:einstein}
    \]
    Inline math is simply \verb|$ a =1 $|, rendered as $a=1$. 
    And we can refer to the labeled entries via \verb|\cref{}|. Let us look at \cref{eq:einstein}.
